Privacy notice

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This notice explains how Aventum International handles personal data that you share with us through this website, in particular through the enquiry form. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who is responsible

Aventum International, a trade name of a sole proprietorship based in Amsterdam, the Netherlands, is the controller of your personal data. You can reach us at info@aventuminternational.com.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we receive the details you enter:

  • name, company and email address;
  • WeChat ID or phone number, if you provide it;
  • details of your enquiry: product, estimated volume, destination port, preferred Incoterm and your message;
  • the language you used on the site.

When you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Why we use it, and on what basis

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry, to prepare a possible agreement and to keep in touch about it. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use your details for newsletters or advertising and we do not sell them.

Who processes your data for us

  • Formspree receives the enquiry form and forwards it to our mailbox. Formspree is based in the United States; transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Netlify hosts this website and, like any web host, processes technical data such as IP addresses in server logs to deliver and secure the site.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface used on this site. When your browser loads it, your IP address is sent to Google.
  • Our email provider stores the messages we receive.

If a business relationship develops, we share only what is needed with the supplier involved in your enquiry, and only with your knowledge.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to two years after our last contact if no agreement follows. If we enter into an agreement, we keep the related records for as long as Dutch tax and accounting law requires (currently seven years).

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies. It remembers your language choice in your browser's local storage. That value stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, ask us to restrict its use, object to it, or request a copy in a portable format. Email info@aventuminternational.com and we will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

We may update this notice when our website or processes change. The date at the top shows the latest version.

隐私声明

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 23 日

本声明说明 Aventum International 如何处理您通过本网站（尤其是询价表单）提供给我们的个人信息。我们收集的信息很少，因此本声明力求简明。

责任方

Aventum International 是一家位于荷兰阿姆斯特丹的个体企业所使用的商号，是您个人信息的控制者。联系邮箱：info@aventuminternational.com。

我们收集哪些信息

当您提交询价时，我们会收到您填写的以下信息：

  • 姓名、公司名称及电子邮箱；
  • 微信号或电话号码（如您提供）；
  • 询价详情：产品、预计数量、目的港、首选贸易术语及留言内容；
  • 您在网站上使用的语言。

如您直接发送电子邮件，我们会收到您的邮箱地址及邮件内容。

使用目的及法律依据

我们仅将上述信息用于回复您的询价、准备可能的合同以及就此与您保持联系。法律依据为：应您的要求在签订合同前采取相关步骤（《通用数据保护条例》（GDPR）第 6 条第 1 款 b 项），以及我们回复商业询价的正当利益（GDPR 第 6 条第 1 款 f 项）。我们不会将您的信息用于新闻邮件或广告，也不会出售您的信息。

代我们处理数据的服务商

  • Formspree：接收询价表单并转发至我们的邮箱。Formspree 位于美国，相关数据传输受欧盟标准合同条款等适当保障措施约束。
  • Netlify：托管本网站。与所有网站托管服务一样，其会在服务器日志中处理 IP 地址等技术数据，以提供并保障网站安全。
  • Google Fonts：提供本网站使用的字体。您的浏览器加载字体时，您的 IP 地址会发送给 Google。
  • 我们的电子邮件服务商：存储我们收到的邮件。

如双方建立业务关系，我们仅会在您知情的情况下，向与您询价相关的供应商提供必要信息。

保存期限

如未达成合作，我们会在最后一次联系后最多保存询价信息两年。如签订合同，相关记录将按照荷兰税务及会计法律的要求保存（目前为七年）。

Cookie 与跟踪

本网站不使用任何分析、跟踪或广告类 Cookie。网站会在您浏览器的本地存储中记住您选择的语言，该信息仅保存在您的设备上，不会发送给我们。

您的权利

您可以要求查阅、更正或删除您的个人信息，要求限制或反对我们使用这些信息，或要求以可携带格式获取副本。请发送邮件至 info@aventuminternational.com，我们将在一个月内答复。您也有权向荷兰数据保护局（Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens）提出投诉。

变更

当网站或处理流程发生变化时，我们可能会更新本声明。页首日期为最新版本日期。
