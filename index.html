How It Works合作流程

Simple. Transparent.
No surprises.简单透明，无任何隐患

From first enquiry to delivery at your port — every step documented, verifiable and structured to protect both parties.从首次询价到交货，每一步均有文件记录，可核实，保障双方利益。

1

Enquiry & NDA询价与保密协议

Submit your enquiry. We issue an NDA to protect all parties — including our supply chain. All information treated with strict confidentiality.提交询价，我们签发保密协议保护各方利益，包括我们的供应链，所有信息严格保密。

2

Letter of Intent (LOI)意向书

You submit a formal LOI confirming product, volume, destination port and payment terms. This allows us to confirm supply availability on our end.您提交正式意向书，确认产品、数量、目的港和付款条款，我们随即确认供应可用性。

3

Sales & Purchase Agreement买卖合同

We issue a formal SPA covering all terms. Payment via confirmed, irrevocable L/C — funds released only on presentation of full shipping documentation.我们签发涵盖所有条款的正式合同，付款通过不可撤销信用证进行，提交完整装运文件后放款。

4

SGS Inspection & Shipment检验与装运

Independent SGS or Intertek inspection at the Brazilian loading port. Full document package: B/L, COO, SGS Report, Phytosanitary Certificate, Packing List.在巴西装货港进行SGS独立检验，出具完整文件包：提单、原产地证书、SGS报告、植物检疫证书、装箱单。

5

Delivery & Ongoing Supply交货与持续供应

Trial shipment delivered to your port. Upon satisfaction, long-term monthly supply commences. We aim to be your permanent Brazilian sourcing partner.试单交货至您的港口，确认满意后开始长期月度供应，致力于成为您永久的巴西采购合作伙伴。
