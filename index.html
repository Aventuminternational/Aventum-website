Aventum does not own the cargo. We act as an independent intermediary between buyer and supplier.Aventum 不拥有货物，我们作为买卖双方之间的独立中间方提供服务。

  1. 01

    Enquiry & NDA询价与保密协议

    Tell us the product, volume, destination port and preferred Incoterm. Where needed, both sides sign an NDA before details are exchanged.请告知产品、数量、目的港及首选贸易术语。如有需要，双方在交换详细信息前签署保密协议。

  2. 02

    Letter of Intent (LOI)意向书（LOI）

    You send a formal LOI on company letterhead. We check it against supplier availability before anything else happens.您以公司抬头纸出具正式意向书。我们据此与供应商核对供货情况，再推进后续步骤。

  3. 03

    Offer & contract (SPA)报价与合同（SPA）

    The supplier confirms price and terms in a formal offer. Once agreed, the Sales & Purchase Agreement fixes specification, volume, inspection, Incoterm and payment.供应商以正式报价确认价格和条款。双方达成一致后，签订买卖合同（SPA），明确规格、数量、检验、贸易术语及付款方式。

  4. 04

    Payment instrument开立付款工具

    The importer opens the agreed payment instrument (L/C preferred), with payment against shipping documents.进口商开立约定的付款工具（优先信用证），凭装运单据付款。

  5. 05

    Inspection & shipment检验与装运

    Independent inspection at the loading port, then loading and departure. You receive the full set: commercial invoice, bill of lading, inspection report, certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate and packing list.在装货港完成独立检验后装船启运。您将收到全套单据：商业发票、提单、检验报告、原产地证书、植物检疫证书及装箱单。

  6. 06

    Delivery & ongoing supply交货与持续供应

    After a successful trial shipment, we move to regular monthly supply on agreed terms.试单顺利完成后，按约定条款转入定期月度供应。

08FAQ常见问题

Questions buyers often ask.买家常见问题。

Can't find your answer? Email us at info@aventuminternational.com.没有找到答案？请发送邮件至 info@aventuminternational.com。

Is there a minimum order?是否有最低起订量？

No fixed minimum. Monthly capacity of up to 50,000+ MT is what our supplier can deliver, not a minimum order. Trial shipments are possible; size and terms are agreed together.没有固定的最低起订量。每月最高 50,000+ 吨是供应商的可供应能力，并非最低起订量。可安排试单，数量及条款由双方协商确定。

Do you only work with buyers in China?你们只与中国买家合作吗？

No. China is our main market, but we also work with importers elsewhere. Send us your destination port and we will check the options with our supplier.不是。中国是我们的主要市场，但我们也与其他国家和地区的进口商合作。请告知您的目的港，我们将与供应商核实可行方案。

Which Incoterms do you offer?提供哪些贸易术语？

FOB Brazilian port is the standard. On request we quote CIF to a named destination port, for example CIF Shanghai Port. The Incoterm is fixed in the contract.标准条款为巴西港口 FOB。如有需要，我们可报指定目的港的 CIF 价格，例如 CIF 上海港。贸易术语以合同为准。

How does payment work?如何付款？

Our preferred instrument is a confirmed, irrevocable Letter of Credit, with payment against shipping documents. Other terms are negotiable and are fixed in the contract.我们优先采用保兑不可撤销信用证，凭装运单据付款。其他付款方式可协商，并写入合同。

How is quality verified?如何核实质量？

By an independent inspector, for example SGS, at the loading port, as agreed in the contract. You receive the inspection report together with the other shipping documents.按合同约定，由独立检验机构（如 SGS）在装货港进行检验。检验报告将与其他装运单据一并提供给您。

Who arranges import licences and registration?谁负责进口许可及注册？

The importer. Depending on the destination this can include an import licence, quota and food-safety registration; in China, for example, TRQ quota and GACC compliance under Decree 280. The supplier provides the export documents.由进口商负责。视目的国而定，可能包括进口许可证、配额及食品安全注册；例如在中国，需办理关税配额（TRQ）并符合海关总署第 280 号令的要求。出口单据由供应商提供。

What exactly does Aventum do?Aventum 具体做什么？

We are an independent broker based in Amsterdam and we do not own the cargo. We match your requirements with supply, coordinate the LOI, offer and contract, and follow up inspection and documents until delivery.我们是总部位于阿姆斯特丹的独立经纪方，不拥有货物。我们负责将您的需求与货源匹配，协调意向书、报价及合同，并跟进检验与单据，直至交货。
